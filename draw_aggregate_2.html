Sphere Aggregate — Help

Draw chains of spheres to build a 3D aggregate. Rotate between strokes to grow arms in different directions. Your work is saved automatically in this browser.

Drawing

A stroke becomes a chain of spheres when you lift. A single tap places one sphere. A ring shows the size of the spheres you are about to draw.

If a stroke starts or ends on an existing sphere, it connects to that sphere at its 3D depth. If both ends hit existing spheres, the chain forms a genuine 3D bridge. A stroke that starts on empty background is drawn in the plane through the aggregate's center.

Moving the view

MobileTwo fingers: drag to rotate, pinch to zoom (around your fingers), twist to roll. Three fingers: pan. Works in any mode.
DesktopRight-drag rotates. Middle-drag or Shift+drag pans. Wheel zooms around the cursor.

Rotation is always around the aggregate's center of mass, so the structure stays put while you turn it.

Modes

Draw, Rotate, Pan set what one finger or left-click does.

Stylus draws, fingers rotate (in the … menu) makes a pen always draw and fingers rotate (or pan, in Pan mode). Touches from a resting palm are ignored while the pen is down.

Sliders

Size — radius of new spheres (in model units, so it is relative to the aggregate, not the screen). Existing spheres keep their size.

Temp — random position deviation from the drawn line. Chain connectivity is preserved.

Spread — random log-normal variation of grain size within a stroke.

Depth — strength of the atmospheric depth cue. Visual only.

Buttons

Undo / Redo — step through strokes.
← — back to the app menu. Hide — hides all controls for a clean view.
Top readout — sphere count and radius of gyration Rg (mass-weighted, in the same model units as the export). On desktop, hover it to see Rg divided by the mean radius.

… menu

Fit to view centers and scales the aggregate to the screen. Reset view also resets rotation.
Colors — background (including transparent) and sphere color.
Share image — preview with perspective; optionally crop to the structure and render at 1×–3× resolution. Shares a PNG via the system sheet on mobile, downloads on desktop.
Export .txt — first line is the sphere count; each following line is index radius x y z. By default y is flipped so the coordinates are right-handed (switch off in the menu if you need raw screen orientation).
Import .txt — loads a file in that same format (replaces the current aggregate after confirmation).
Clear all — removes everything (with confirmation).
