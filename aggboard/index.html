AggBoard

A notebook whiteboard with a built-in 3D fractal aggregate generator. Everything is saved automatically on this device.

Drawing

Pen draws (pressure-sensitive with Apple Pencil). Eraser removes whole strokes it touches. Select: draw a loop around strokes or aggregates, then drag inside the frame to move them, drag a corner square to scale the group (handwriting and aggregates keep their proportions; aggregates keep their exact structure), or use Duplicate / Delete. Tapping a colour recolours selected strokes. Tap a single item with Select to pick it. Laser draws a red trail that fades — for pointing while presenting.

Straight lines and shapes

Draw as usual, then keep the pencil still for half a second at the end of the stroke. It turns into the shape it looks like: a straight line (exactly horizontal, vertical or 45° when close), an angled line such as an L for axes, an arrow (draw the shaft with a quick head in one stroke, or draw a line and then a “V” at its end), a triangle, a rectangle (squared up), another polygon, or a circle or ellipse. You see the shape while still holding; lift to keep it, or move on to keep the freehand stroke.

Pencil and fingers

Once an Apple Pencil is detected, the pencil draws and fingers pan and zoom (one finger pans, two fingers pinch). Your palm is ignored while the pencil touches the screen. Without a pencil, one finger draws and two fingers pan and zoom. You can force either behaviour under … → Finger input.

Aggregates

Choose the aggregate tool and drag a box. When you lift, the box fills with a 3D fractal aggregate, and the tool switches back to the pen.

Tap an aggregate (with the pencil, a finger or the mouse, in any tool except the laser) to select it; then tap Adjust, or simply double-tap the aggregate, to open its settings:

Monomer size — the monomer radius. The number of monomers changes so the aggregate still fills its box (up to 5000 monomers).
Fractal dimension — from 1 (a straight chain) to 3 (compact). The aggregate is regenerated as you drag.
New random — same settings, different random structure.
Advanced — the prefactor kf in N = kf (Rg/a)Df. Auto chooses a value that works for every Df.

When an aggregate is selected: drag it to move. The handles: bottom-right scales it (bigger or smaller, same structure, so the monomers scale too); bottom-left (dashed box) changes the box and regenerates the aggregate to fill it, keeping the monomer size, so the number of monomers changes; top-right (round arrow) rotates it in 3D. Chains and edited aggregates have no bottom-left handle, because they cannot be regenerated. With fingers in pan-and-zoom mode, dragging one finger on a selected aggregate also rotates it.

The readout shows N, the radius of gyration Rg (in monomer radii a), and Df fitted from the growth history as a check.

Particle chains

The chain tool (spheres without a box) turns a stroke into touching spheres. It uses the current monomer size and the last temperature you set. Temperature makes the chain wiggle in 3D; rotate it to see.

Start or end a chain on a sphere of an aggregate and it attaches there, at that sphere's depth, and becomes part of the aggregate. Start on one aggregate and end on another to bridge them into one (both need the same monomer size). This works the same with chains: branch off a chain, connect two chains, or close a chain into a ring. New spheres touch where they join and steer around existing spheres instead of overlapping them.

An aggregate with hand-drawn parts is marked edited: it no longer regenerates, its monomer-size slider and corner handle scale it, and Regenerate… turns it back into a fresh aggregate (removing the drawn parts). Double-tap a standalone chain to change its monomer size, temperature or random pattern; it is re-laid along the line you drew.

Pages

Use the arrows at the bottom to move between pages and + to add a page after the current one. … → Clear this page removes everything on the page in one step (Undo brings it back). Undo works per page and covers drawing, erasing, moving and all aggregate changes.

Keeping your notes

Notebooks live in this browser on this device. On iPad, add AggBoard to your Home Screen, otherwise Safari may delete its data after a week without use. Use … → Save notebook to file regularly as a backup, or to give a notebook to someone else; they open it with Open notebook from file.

Keyboard

P pen · E eraser · S select · L laser · A aggregate · C chain · 1–5 colours · Ctrl/⌘ Z undo · Ctrl/⌘ Shift Z redo · Ctrl/⌘ D duplicate · Delete delete selection · PgUp/PgDn pages · 0 fit page · Esc deselect

Mouse: right- or middle-drag pans, Ctrl + wheel (or trackpad pinch) zooms, wheel scrolls.
