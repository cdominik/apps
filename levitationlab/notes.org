* Levitation Lab Ideas

** HIGH — Deepen the core physics loop

*** TODO Live orbit centre dot on the equator per levitated particle
A single glowing dot at x_c = v_t/omega per levitated particle, visible
without enabling the full encounter overlay. Makes the fundamental equation
tangible for casual players, not just experts.

*** TODO Visual flash when a particle first enters the levitation zone
Currently there is a chime but no visual cue on the drum itself. A brief
ring flash at the particle's orbit radius would make the "aha" moment much
clearer for new players.

*** TODO Shaded band on the equator showing the spread of orbit centres
A semi-transparent band from v_t,min/omega to v_t,max/omega when spread > 0.
Makes it immediately obvious why spread is needed for aggregate formation —
particles need to cross each other's orbits.

** HIGH — Game completeness

*** TODO Hint system for post-game free sandbox
After completing the game, players are dropped into free play with no
guidance. A lightweight hint system — a small text ticker that notices
what is happening and comments on it — would bridge the gap between
game and expert mode.

*** TODO Replay / slow-motion moment capture
When something dramatic happens (aggregate forms, pebble spawns), save
the last 3 seconds of physics state and offer to replay it in slow motion.
Scientifically meaningful — these events are rare and fast.

** MEDIUM — Expert tools

*** TODO Stokes number live readout
A live readout of St = v_t * omega / g or similar, showing where particles
sit in the aerodynamic regime. Connects the expert panel directly to the
published literature.

*** TODO Collision rate history chart in viewport
A rolling time series of aggregate-aggregate close approaches per revolution.
Makes the connection between spread, drum speed, and collision frequency
visible as a graph rather than just a heatmap.

*** TODO Freeze-and-inspect mode
Pause and tap any particle to see its v_t, orbit centre, orbit radius, and
levitation status in a small tooltip. Most of the data is already computed.

*** TODO Export current state as JSON snapshot
Positions, velocities, v_t values. Allows researchers to hand off interesting
configurations to other simulation tools or share them.

** MEDIUM — Presentation and outreach

*** TODO Real microscope image / video in viewport triggered by aggregate formation
The rule engine is already built for exactly this. Infrastructure is in place;
needs lab content.

*** TODO Animated v_t distribution narrowing over time
A slow animated overlay showing the v_t distribution narrowing as the drum
selects for a particular size range. This is the core physical message of
the experiment and is not currently shown dynamically.

*** TODO Network leaderboard for challenge mode
Even a simple read/write to a public JSON endpoint would make outreach events
far more engaging — visitors competing against each other on a shared screen.
Currently scores are localStorage only.

** LOW — Polish and feel

*** TODO Haptic feedback on mobile
A short vibration pulse when a particle is lost or levitated. Almost free
to implement, adds physical presence on mobile devices.

*** TODO Tray collection as a scored challenge outcome
Currently the tray is a neutral collection tool. Giving it a challenge score
(particles collected × size bonus) would give it game relevance.

*** TODO Wind-down animation when drum stops
Particles slowly settling to the bottom, aggregates sliding down the wall.
Currently everything freezes. A few seconds of graceful settling would make
the physics feel complete.
